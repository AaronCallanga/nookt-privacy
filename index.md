# Nookt privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Nookt is a document and login vault that keeps everything on your phone. This policy explains what that means.

## What we collect

Nothing. Nookt has no account, no server, and no internet permission. We never receive your documents, logins, notes, contacts, or any information about how you use the app.

## Where your data lives

- Everything you add is encrypted and stored only on your phone.
- We cannot open, read, recover, or reset your vault.
- If you save a backup file, it is encrypted and goes only where you choose (for example your own Google Drive or computer). We never receive it.

## Services from Google that Nookt uses on your phone

- **Document scanning** runs on your phone through Google Play services (ML Kit document scanner).
- **Purchases** are handled by Google Play. Google processes your payment under its own privacy policy. Nookt only learns whether you have an active purchase.

## Notifications

Expiry reminders are created on your phone. They never say which document is expiring.

## Children

Nookt is meant for adults (18+).
