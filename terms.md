Last updated: 17 November 2026

These terms cover your use of the Nookt app. By using Nookt, you agree to them.

## What Nookt is

Nookt stores your documents, logins and notes on your phone, encrypted. There is no account and no Nookt server.

## Your data is yours, and so is the responsibility

- Everything you add stays on your phone. We never receive it.
- We cannot open, recover or reset your vault.
- If you lose both your passphrase and your recovery code, your data cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.
- If your phone is lost, broken or reset, your data is gone unless you saved a backup file.
- Keep your Emergency Kit and backup file somewhere safe.

## Free trial and paid plans

- New vaults get 30 days of full access for free.
- After 30 days, your vault stays readable, searchable and exportable. Adding and editing need a paid plan.
- **Lifetime** is a one-time payment for the app, including future features.
- **Yearly** renews every year until you cancel it in Google Play. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the paid year ends.
- Google Play handles all payments, renewals and refunds under its own policies.
